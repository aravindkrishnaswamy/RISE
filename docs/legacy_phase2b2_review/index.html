Legacy deprecation Phase 2 slice B2: look review

102 rows: the 101 scenes migrated off the remaining legacy chunk families that render here, plus translucent_bunny (kept by owner ruling; 102 scenes are migrated in all, the 102nd being scenes/pr.RISEscene, see below), by tools/migrate_scenes_legacy_families.py: the seven frozen legacy BRDF materials (SCENE_CONVENTIONS 11.5 table), iridescent_painter (thin-film ggx_material), ambient_light (a uniform radiance_map of radiance color x power / pi) and MLT (BDPT; VCM for the four SDS-caustic showcases). "Before" is the scene at master 5bd23470d; "migrated" is the tool's output; the third column is the owner-precedent retune where one was applied, or a measured proposal where an authored intent is at stake. All renders at reduced resolution (long side 160 px) with the shipped sample counts, three Sobol' value salts each (the three Benchmarks: one); thumbnails are salt 1, the PRE-denoise image composited over black, linear Rec.709 clamped to [0,1] and sRGB-encoded. The fourth column is the final image as OIDN denoises it, for FeatureBased showcases (denoising stays on as authored). Mean Y is the Rec.709 luminance of the raw image, mean over the salts (± salt-to-salt sd). 16 scenes change by more than 10% (final vs before); each carries a measured cause. "noisy" flags a salt sd above 5% of the mean. scenes/pr.RISEscene (performance rating) is migrated but not shown: it does not load outside its own working directory (missing ../models meshes) before or after.

How to read the large changes

  • Energy the legacy models invented. White-furnace spheres (uniform radiance 1, PT 256 spp): a Phong 0.5 / white / N 60 reads 1.246, the glass_pavilion Cook-Torrance bronze RGB 1.18 / 0.86 / 0.55 (its diffuse is added uncoupled to the conductor lobe), against energy-bounded GGX twins. A polished_material over a 0.5 substrate reads 0.526 against 0.327 for coated_material: the legacy model renormalises its substrate by the coat's mean transmittance, so it never loses the light the coat traps by internal reflection (and at ior 10000 it reflects ~2x: full substrate plus a ~100% coat).
  • Ambient was unoccluded. ambient_light added color x power x f(n, n) everywhere, including inside a cloud and on mirrors (where f(n, n) is tiny); the uniform environment is occluded and is seen in reflections.
  • Owner-precedent retunes applied (slice B1: a showcase is retuned back to its legacy mean when the cause is a legacy bias or fudge): cloud_showcase, glass_pavilion, pavilion_colonnade, pillow, and the test caustic_sss (whose B1 spot retune assumed the ambient term stays dropped). Each is within 0.5% of its legacy mean and carries a scene comment.
  • Owner rulings 2026-10-10 (applied): translucent_bunny KEEPS the deprecated iridescent_painter (translucent_material has no thin film; legacy look, +0.0%); both teapots use a 125 nm titanium film that keeps the purple look (+25.0% showcase: the film's physical grazing Fresnel, not a legacy bias, so no light retune); wetness_prelude_validation's -11.6% accepted as is.
  • Not migrated: the Android catalogue scenes held for slice C (kaleidoscope_atrium, tidepools, pt_jewel_vault; shapes), and the three Cook-Torrance subject scenes (cooktorrance_multiscatter_test, cooktorrance_vndf_test, ggx_vs_cooktorrance), which moved unmigrated into scenes/Tests/Legacy/.
