Legacy deprecation Phase 2 slice B1: look review

44 scenes moved off pixelpel_rasterizer / pixelintegratingspectral_rasterizer by tools/migrate_scenes_legacy_rasterizer.py --mode full: photon map / final gather / irradiance cache, point-cloud SSS, Whitted, distribution-tracing and area-light-op chains. "Before" is the scene at master 83aa86d2f; "after" is the migrated scene. Both are rendered at reduced resolution (long side 160 px) with the shipped sample counts, three Sobol' value salts each (blurry_floor eight, photon_cloister twelve); the thumbnails are salt 1, the PRE-denoise image composited over black, linear Rec.709 clamped to [0,1] and sRGB-encoded identically. The third column is the after-image as OIDN denoises it (shown for FeatureBased showcases, which keep denoising on as authored). Mean Y is the Rec.709 luminance of the composited raw image, mean over the salts (± salt-to-salt sd; the unclamped mean, so a blown-out image reads far above 1). 7 scenes change by more than 10%; each carries a measured cause. "noisy" flags a salt sd above 5% of the mean.

How to read the large changes

  • Fudge factors removed. The point-cloud SSS ops scaled their glow by irrad_scale and the photon maps their caustics by power_scale; neither is physical. Doubling a scene's irrad_scale moves its legacy mean linearly (measured per scene), so those looks were tuned, and the physical replacements (subsurfacescattering_material, VCM) come out brighter or darker by whatever the fudge was. The owner-approved light retunes that restore each mean are applied (see below).
  • Truncated or missing transport restored. The legacy chains had no indirect diffuse op (Whitted, glossy-only DT, AO), truncated it (final gather, photon maps), or had no direct lighting at all (diacaustic, motion_blurred_caustic). Measured by rendering PT with max_diffuse_bounce 0 / 1 / 2 against the legacy image.
  • Not migrated: different_rmaps — per-OBJECT radiance_map is FROZEN (owner-approved): only the legacy shader-op chain honours it, while the path tracer, BDPT and VCM read only the rasterizer's map, and a load warning now says so. The scene moved unmigrated into the legacy corpus, scenes/Tests/Legacy/different_rmaps.RISEscene, where its legacy render is hash-pinned. Also left: visiblehuman (frozen DVR), shapes and kaleidoscope_atrium (slice C).
  • Owner-approved retunes (2026-10-10) were applied to the twelve scenes whose fudge factors or truncation had moved them most; each is back within 1% of its legacy mean (the "migrated, before retune" column shows where the bare migration landed). The after-images are the retuned scenes.
