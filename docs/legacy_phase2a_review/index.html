Legacy deprecation Phase 2a: look review

107 scenes moved off pixelpel_rasterizer / pixelintegratingspectral_rasterizer by tools/migrate_scenes_legacy_rasterizer.py. "Before" is the scene at master 6ff502457; "after" is the migrated scene. Both are rendered at reduced resolution (long side 160 px) with the shipped sample counts, three Sobol' value salts each; the thumbnail is salt 1, the PRE-denoise image composited over black (RGB × coverage alpha: the legacy pixel rasterizers store surface RGB plus coverage alpha, so raw RGB differs at silhouettes), linear Rec.709 clamped to [0,1] and sRGB-encoded identically on both sides. Mean Y is the Rec.709 luminance of that composited raw image, mean over the three salts (± is the salt-to-salt standard deviation). 5 scenes change by more than 10%. Path-tracing chains (--mode pt) should match up to noise; direct-lighting chains (--mode direct, owner ruling #3a, max_diffuse_bounce 0) are expected to change: specular paths and MIS-weighted emitter hits now contribute.

What the large changes are

  • spectral_skinmodel, spectral_skin_fast (+961%, +571%): both set show_luminaires FALSE. The legacy shader-dispatch path hid the emitter from EVERY ray, not just camera rays, so the skin (a BSDF-less material lit only by BSDF-sampled continuations hitting the emitter) received almost no light. Cross-check: the legacy render with show_luminaires TRUE reads 1.1505 against the path tracer's 1.1504 with FALSE. The migrated image is the correct one.
  • dielectrics_changing_ior / _scat (exactly -50%): their advanced_shader runs two path-tracing ops over overlapping depth ranges (0 1 + and 1 10 +), which the legacy chain summed: a double count. The path tracer counts once. The tool reports this as a look change.
  • gltf_import_damaged_helmet_spectral (+97%) and gltf_import_damaged_helmet: firefly-dominated at 16 spp (salt-to-salt sd 23% / 9%); individual 256-spp renders of the same scene swing from 0.033 to 0.47. Not a measurable change. Separately, its ambient_light-only contribution reads about 2x higher under the legacy chain than under the path tracer (ambient_light is deprecated and moves to a radiance_map in slice B).
  • Everything else is within 10%. Direct-lighting scenes typically move a few percent (max_diffuse_bounce 0 still adds specular paths and MIS-weighted emitter hits); a direct-only scene of Lambertian spheres under a directional light (Tests/Painters/blackbodyradiator) renders with an identical mean. An earlier pass of this gallery compared RAW RGB and showed spurious 10-33% drops: the legacy pixel rasterizers store surface RGB plus coverage alpha, so only the composite over black is comparable.
