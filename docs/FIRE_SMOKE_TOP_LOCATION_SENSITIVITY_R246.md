# r246 — matched top-location sensitivity (design draft; not implemented)

## Evidence and decision

The full r244 baseline/wide spectra did not establish expected puffing. The
unchanged r245 wide run's full accepted budgets passed byte/arithmetic audit,
then two fresh zero-P1/P2 reviews after a postprocessor input-pin correction.
Its integrated statistics ledger has 26.943% of open-boundary mass inflow at
the top. The subsequent 235-frame spatial map places 95.516% of sampled top
mass inflow at r≥0.60 m and97.569% of sampled outflow at r<0.60 m; outer
endpoint cells are near300 K. These are sampled limited-Heun rates and
separate endpoint values, not integrated interior entrainment, stage donors,
pressure classes or causal proof. See r245's retained small reports.

Choose a **top-position-only experimental comparison** next. Preserve the
§3.6 pressure-open law and §3.7 owner. §3.6 requires checking boundary
placement; the current measurements justify testing its influence rather
than changing the boundary equation. No production domain multiplier changes
at this boundary. Puffing and the soot/tier-10 milestones remain unresolved.

extends across the full taller grid; therefore also retain a separately labeled
**common-height** centerline/reaction reduction over original layers0..79,

Retain accepted scalar ledgers and stage-operand capsules at all235 original
boundaries. Report top radial exchange and signed near-base momentum with the
same semantics as r245; do not integrate sampled momentum across missing steps.

Compare against the frozen r245/r244 wide evidence over the complete identical
physical window. Conclusions about the original-region response require a changed identical-
support common-height signal or original near-base station response. The
full physical preview may establish a rendered optical response, but camera/
film equality does not guarantee equal emitting/absorbing support along rays;
a preview-only change cannot establish original-region dynamics. A change confined to the native160-layer aggregate or larger
whole-domain inventory/side-wall budgets establishes a changed aggregate, not
an original-region response. A synthetic observation control with identical
layers0..79 and added upper-layer reaction/emission/transmittance must prove
this distinction even when an oblique camera ray changes its rendered result.
Neither kind of change by itself proves a faulty pressure-open law. An unchanged
matched response bounds cold-start, fixed-window sensitivity for this tested
extension, not universal boundary independence. The preserved discard spans2.5 new geometric flow-through times rather than
canonical five. The original14.625 s statistics span only about23 cycles at
the observed1.57 Hz peak. These facts prevent a developed taller-domain or
≥30-cycle canonical puffing qualification claim from this diagnostic. The
native expected puffing comparison and original physical gates remain
authoritative; no gate is relaxed to convert a sensitivity result into a pass. Adoption of any production domain change
requires a later numbered evidence-supported decision. If puffing remains
unsettled, use the new budget/station comparison to localize the next remedy;

## Rejected alternatives

- A top pressure equation change from gross inflow alone: peripheral ambient
  exchange is compatible with plume entrainment and does not establish a defect.
- Blind nz doubling under canonical timing: changes pilot/discard clocks and
  loses isolation. Blindly retaining clocks without explicit identity is also
  rejected because it falsely presents a canonical taller case.
- Higher resolution, different lateral extent, source reseeding, a short pilot
  probe or shortened window: confounds the authorized matched comparison.
- Dissipation or pressure-tolerance tuning from sampled term RMS: no causal
  evidence or numerical defect has been established.
