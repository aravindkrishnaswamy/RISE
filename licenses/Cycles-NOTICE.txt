RISE contains code ported from Blender's Cycles renderer (intern/cycles),
Blender commit afc6d7a4 (5.2 alpha, 2026-03-17), in
src/Library/Utilities/OslNoise.h, OslNoise.cpp, OslVoronoi.h,
OslVoronoi.cpp, OslColor.h and OslColor.cpp.  The port was modified for
RISE: it was rewritten as standalone C++ functions, the four
per-dimension overloads of each noise fractal were merged into one, as
were the 2D, 3D and 4D copies of each Voronoi feature, and guards were
added for non-finite coordinates (gradient noise and Voronoi), runaway
detail, Voronoi cell ids beyond the int32 range, and out-of-range
Voronoi feature, metric, dimension or Mix mode values.
