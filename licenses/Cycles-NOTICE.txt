RISE contains code ported from Blender's Cycles renderer (intern/cycles),
Blender commit afc6d7a4 (5.2 alpha, 2026-03-17), in
src/Library/Utilities/OslNoise.h, OslNoise.cpp, OslVoronoi.h,
OslVoronoi.cpp, OslColor.h, OslColor.cpp, OslMath.h, OslMath.cpp,
OslVector.h, OslVector.cpp, OslShaderNodes.h and OslShaderNodes.cpp.
The port was modified for RISE: it was
rewritten as standalone C++ functions, the four per-dimension overloads of
each noise fractal were merged into one, as were the 2D, 3D and 4D copies
of each Voronoi feature, and guards were added for non-finite coordinates
(gradient noise and Voronoi), runaway detail, Voronoi cell ids and
safe_powf exponents beyond the int32 range, and out-of-range Voronoi,
Mix, Math, Vector Math, Clamp, Map Range or Vector Rotate enum values.
