RISE contains code ported from Blender's Cycles renderer (intern/cycles),
Blender commit afc6d7a4 (5.2 alpha, 2026-03-17), in
src/Library/Utilities/OslNoise.h and src/Library/Utilities/OslNoise.cpp.
The port was modified for RISE: it was rewritten as standalone C++
functions, the four per-dimension overloads of each fractal were merged
into one, and guards were added for non-finite coordinates and runaway
detail.
